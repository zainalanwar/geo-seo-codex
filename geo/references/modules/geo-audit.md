# GEO audit

Open the submitted URL and inventory representative pages from navigation and sitemap. Record retrieval time, requested and final URLs, status and evidence gaps. Determine business type from visible evidence. Sample up to 50 same-site canonical URLs, prioritizing high-value navigation, services/products, author and recent editorial pages. If the sitemap is unavailable, use internal links up to two levels deep. For a portfolio, include the homepage, one service and one case study. Reuse page captures across the five specialist reviews in ../specialists/ and the crawler module.

Review search access, HTML/indexability, brand identity, content and structured data. For every actionable issue record its URL, observed evidence, impact, fix and a repeatable verification. Rank by observed effect and scope. Explain which pages were sampled. Use geo-report.md to produce the Markdown deliverable.
