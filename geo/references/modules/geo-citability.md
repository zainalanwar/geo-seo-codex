# GEO citability

Extract passage text with its URL, heading and location. Select passages answering real audience questions. Evaluate whether each passage identifies its subject, gives a direct answer, explains scope and attributes verifiable claims to a dated source. Inspect headings, lists and tables when they aid comprehension.

Quote a brief original and draft a revision for the highest-impact passages. Preserve numbers, qualifications, attribution and intended language. Flag claims needing an author-provided source. Record actual AI citations separately with prompt, provider, observed answer, cited URL and date. Passage length and internal scores do not predict citations.
