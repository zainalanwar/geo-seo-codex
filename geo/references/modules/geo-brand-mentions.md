# GEO brand mentions

Identify brand spellings, official profiles and ambiguous names from first-party pages. Search each confirmed spelling alongside its services and market. Record query, date, locale, source URL, publisher and context for each independently verified mention. Separate owned profiles, independent coverage and citations actually observed in assistant responses. Disambiguate people and companies before counting, and deduplicate syndication.

Build a table of source, ownership, evidence excerpt and identity confidence. List coverage gaps and specific factual corrections or profile improvements. Report an unavailable platform as unobserved. A failed search cannot establish absence across the web.
