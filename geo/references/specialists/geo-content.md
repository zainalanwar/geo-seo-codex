# Content quality

Review representative service, editorial and case-study pages against their purpose and audience. Trace one material claim per page to its source, date, author or supporting example. For case studies, distinguish the client's baseline, intervention, measured result, timeframe and permission to identify the client. When a fact needs confirmation, flag it for the owner.

Identify the passage that directly answers the reader's question and one concrete improvement tied to its wording. Preserve language and topic conventions. Avoid inferring authorship from writing style, treating a word-count target as quality, or presenting an editorial score as Google's assessment.

Return the page, claim, evidence, gap and proposed edit. Apply [the shared rubric](../scoring.md).
